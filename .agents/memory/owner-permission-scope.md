---
name: Owner permission scope
description: The creator's requested administrative scope and limits on applying it to other accounts
---

The creator requested: «حسابي خليه له كل الصلاحيات انا الادمن». This applies to their own account within their organization, the scope offered immediately before their approval.

**Why:** They need the full financial and administrative capabilities, including account deletion, rather than the limited combination of organization administration and ordinary accounting.

**How to apply:** Preserve full in-organization authority for that account without interpreting it as approval for platform-wide Super Admin or changes to every user's shared role defaults. Keep accounting safeguards for used accounts and journal entries. This is not a blanket instruction to grant every newly registered user full permissions.
